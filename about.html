Mission

Evidence on the mechanisms and consequences of digital influence

GCDIR conducts and disseminates research on how digital influence works and what it does. Its objective is to contribute peer-quality empirical evidence to scholarly literature, public policy debate and professional practice.

Academic affiliations ensure that research questions are framed and answered to scholarly standards; industry partnerships provide access to the behavioral data and applied context that make such research tractable. The Center maintains editorial independence from its partners and publishes its findings openly.

What we do

  • Original research on digital influence
  • Methodological development
  • Working papers and peer-reviewed articles
  • Seminars and workshops
  • Collaborative projects between academic researchers and industry data providers

Origins and independence

GCDIR was established in 2025 by researchers and practitioners working at the intersection of behavioral science and the digital influence industry.

Several members of the Center’s leadership hold concurrent roles at Trudy, which is also one of GCDIR’s industry data partners. The Center maintains editorial independence in the design, conduct and publication of its research, and discloses funding sources, data provenance and relevant affiliations in all publications, in accordance with standard academic practice.

Institutional model

GCDIR operates as a collaborative research network rather than as a hosted institute. It is not affiliated with any single university or commercial entity.

The Center maintains formal partnerships with academic institutions and industry organizations that provide data access and applied context. Its people, governance and partners are described on the People page and below.
