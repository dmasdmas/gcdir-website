Mission

GCDIR studies how social media creators persuade their audiences, and what measurable effects that persuasion has. It publishes its results in working papers and journals, and presents them at seminars and at the GCDIR Symposium.

Academic members set the research questions and methods. Industry partners provide data and context from commercial campaigns, but do not decide what is studied or published. The rules are set out on the independence and integrity page.

Activities

  • Original research on persuasion and influence
  • Methods for measuring influence across millions of creators
  • Working papers and journal articles
  • Seminars and the GCDIR Symposium
  • Joint projects between academic researchers and data partners

Relationship with Trudy

Several GCDIR members also work at Trudy, which provides the Center’s main data resource. The Center decides independently what it studies and publishes, and every publication discloses funding, data sources and the authors’ affiliations.

Institutional model

GCDIR is a research network. Its members work from their own institutions, and it has no host university. It works with universities through its members’ affiliations, and with companies that provide data. Its members and advisors are listed on the People page.
