Why we exist

Much of influencer marketing is still a guess.

Brands spend a great deal deciding which creators to trust with their name. Researchers have had little better to go on, because the records that would settle the question sit inside companies.

We study how creators persuade their audiences and measure what that persuasion does. We publish the results in working papers and journals, and present them at seminars and at the GCDIR Symposium.

Academic members set the questions and the methods. Industry partners provide data and context from commercial campaigns. They don’t decide what we study or what we publish, and the rules that make sure of it are on the independence and integrity page.

What we do

  • Original research on persuasion and influence
  • Methods for measuring influence across millions of creators
  • Working papers and journal articles
  • Seminars and the GCDIR Symposium
  • Joint projects between academic researchers and data partners

Our relationship with Trudy

Trudy provides our main data resource, and several GCDIR members also work at Trudy. We decide on our own what we study and publish. Every publication discloses its funding, its data sources and its authors’ affiliations.

Who holds which role

How we’re organized

GCDIR is a research network. Members work from their own institutions, and there is no host university. We work with universities through our members’ affiliations, and with companies that provide data.
