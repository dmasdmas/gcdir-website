Leadership

Magnus Hansson, PhD

Director

Sets the Center’s research agenda. Affiliated with Stockholm University. Founding Lead Data Scientist at Trudy.

Didrik Svendsen

Head of Strategy and Partnerships

Responsible for partnerships and data access. CEO and co-founder of Trudy.

Advisory board

The advisory board advises the Director on the research agenda and on industry partnerships.

Ryan Schram

Advisor, Creator Economy

Executive Director of the Center for the Creator Economy at Syracuse University. Previously 13 years at IZEA, one of the first influencer marketing platforms, latterly as President and Chief Operating Officer.

Ian Randolph

Advisor, Behavioral Science

Works on AI and data products grounded in psychology. BA in Cognitive Science, Yale (summa cum laude); MSc in Decision Sciences, London School of Economics. Previously modelled social unrest from social media data for a DARPA research program, and was Head of Product and R&D at Tailify.

Philip Madero Hammarskiöld

Industry Advisor

Advises on industry partnerships and applied research. Co-founder of Trudy.
