Leadership

Magnus Hansson, PhD

Director

Leads the Center and sets its research agenda and scientific direction. Affiliated with Stockholm University. Concurrently Founding Lead Data Scientist at Trudy.

Didrik Svendsen

Head of Strategy and Partnerships

Responsible for the Center’s institutional development, partnerships and data access. Concurrently CEO and co-founder of Trudy.

Advisory board

The advisory board guides the Center’s long-term research agenda and the practical relevance of its work in academic and industry contexts.

Ryan Schram

Advisor, Creator Economy

Executive Director of the Center for the Creator Economy at Syracuse University. Previously President and Chief Operating Officer of IZEA, where he spent 13 years helping build one of the first influencer marketing platforms.

Ian Randolph

Advisor, Behavioral Science

Builds AI and data products at the intersection of psychology and data science. BA in Cognitive Science from Yale (summa cum laude) and MSc in Decision Sciences from the London School of Economics (top graduate). Began his career modelling social unrest from social media data for a DARPA research program; formerly Head of Product and R&D at Tailify. Has lectured on data science and ethics at more than 15 public events.

Philip Madero Hammarskiöld

Industry Advisor

Provides advisory input on partnerships and applied research directions. Co-founder of Trudy.
