How applications are assessed

  1. You apply. Choose the track that fits best. One application per project is enough.
  2. We review. The research team assesses fit with the research agenda and feasibility, and for data access, whether the question can be answered with the available data within our data-protection rules.
  3. We talk. Shortlisted applicants are invited to a call with a member of the team.
  4. We agree terms. Data access and affiliations start with a written agreement covering data use, publication and confidentiality.

Good to know

Positions in the network are currently voluntary and unpaid. All research follows our independence and integrity commitments.
