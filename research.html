Main program

The psychology of persuasion in creator-mediated environments

The program asks when content from individual creators on social platforms changes what their audiences think and do, and which creators are most persuasive.

It is built on the Center’s main data resource, which links psychological profiles of creators to the conversions and revenue from their campaigns. The Data page describes what the resource contains and how access works.

Research questions

  1. Which psychological characteristics of creators, such as values, personality and communication style, predict how their audiences respond?
  2. How do audiences come to trust creators they know only through a feed, and how does that trust affect persuasion?
  3. Does similarity between a creator and their audience increase engagement and conversions, and when does it stop helping?
  4. When does exposure to creator content lead to a measurable change in behavior, commercial or otherwise?
  5. How can psychological influence be measured reliably across millions of creators and campaigns?
