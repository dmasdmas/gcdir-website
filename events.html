The first GCDIR Symposium brings together academic researchers with people who work at platforms, brands and creator businesses. The program is for empirical work: studies that test how persuasion and trust operate on social platforms, ideally against measured outcomes such as purchases or sign-ups.

Program

The program will consist of invited keynotes, talks selected from submitted proposals, and a closing discussion between researchers and practitioners.

Speakers and the full program will be published on this page.

  1. Creator characteristics and audience response
  2. Measuring influence by outcomes such as purchases and changes in behavior
  3. Similarity between creators and their audiences, and when it helps or hurts
  4. Misinformation in feed-based media
  5. AI-generated creators and content
  6. Pre-registration, causal inference and measurement in large observational data
