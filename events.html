The first GCDIR Symposium brings together academic researchers and the people who build, fund and work inside creator-driven media. Its focus is empirical: work that tests how persuasion, trust and behavioral change happen on social platforms, measured against real outcomes rather than engagement proxies.

Program

Themes

The program is built from invited keynotes and talks selected from submitted proposals, with a closing discussion between researchers and practitioners from platforms, brands and creator businesses.

Speakers and the full program will be published on this page.

  1. Persuasion and trust in creator-mediated environments: which creator characteristics move audiences, and why
  2. Measuring influence by outcomes: conversions, purchases and behavior change instead of likes and impressions
  3. Congruency between creators and audiences, and when similarity helps or hurts
  4. Misinformation and persuasive integrity in feed-based media
  5. AI-generated creators and content, and what they change about influence
  6. Methods: pre-registration, causal inference and measurement at platform scale
