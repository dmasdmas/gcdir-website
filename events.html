The first GCDIR Symposium brings academic researchers together with people who work at platforms, brands and creator businesses.

The program is for empirical work: studies that test how persuasion and trust work on social platforms, ideally against measured outcomes such as purchases or sign-ups. Theory is welcome too, if it is willing to meet the data.

Program

What we’ll talk about.

Invited keynotes, talks chosen from submitted proposals, and a closing discussion between researchers and practitioners.

Speakers and the full program will be posted on this page.

  1. Creator characteristics and how audiences respond
  2. Measuring influence by outcomes, such as purchases and changes in behavior
  3. Similarity between creators and audiences, and when it helps or hurts
  4. Misinformation in feed-based media
  5. AI-generated creators and content
  6. Pre-registration, causal inference and measurement in large observational data
