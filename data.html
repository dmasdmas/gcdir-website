What the data record

For each campaign, the data record what the audience did afterwards: conversions, revenue and related transactions. Each campaign is linked to a profile of the creator who ran it.

Research on influence has mostly relied on likes, shares and views, because those are public. Records of purchases and sign-ups are held by brands and platforms; to our knowledge, few academic studies have had access to them at this scale.

The data are harmonized across brands, so results can be compared across companies and product categories, which the data of a single brand cannot support.

Contents

Campaigns
About 230,000 commercial influencer campaigns with outcome data, from more than 800 brands in consumer categories
Creators
Psychometric and behavioral profiles of about 10 million content creators on the major platforms and markets
Measures
About 160 standardized signals per profiled creator, including personality, values, communication style and audience characteristics
Period
More than ten years of campaigns, harmonized to a common measurement framework
Outcomes
Conversions, revenue and related transaction-level records

Access

GCDIR researchers and approved external collaborators can request access to the data for a defined project.

Apply for data access

Project-based access

Access is granted for a named project with a stated research question.

Review

Each request goes through the Center’s data governance and ethics review before any data are shared.

Access policy

A written access policy for external researchers, including the procedure for institutional review, is in preparation.

Disclosure

The data are provided by Trudy, and several GCDIR members also work at Trudy. Each publication states its funding, where its data came from and the authors’ affiliations. See independence and integrity.
