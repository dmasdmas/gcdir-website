Why it matters

Measured behavior, not engagement proxies

A central feature of the dataset is that creator-level psychometric profiles are linked to measured downstream behavioral outcomes from commercial campaigns, including conversions, revenue and related transaction-level data, rather than to likes, shares or impressions.

This makes it possible to investigate questions about persuasion, trust and behavioral change that are difficult to address with publicly available data.

The dataset is harmonized across contributors, allowing comparative analysis that is generally not possible with the proprietary data of any single brand or platform.

The principal data resource

  • {% include icons/chart.svg %}Campaign-level outcome data, including conversions and revenue, from approximately 230,000 commercial influencer campaigns contributed by more than 800 brands across consumer categories
  • {% include icons/person.svg %}Psychometric and behavioral profiles of approximately 10 million content creators across major platforms and markets
  • {% include icons/database.svg %}Approximately 160 standardized signals per profiled creator, including personality, values, communication style and audience characteristics
  • {% include icons/doc.svg %}A campaign history spanning more than a decade, collected under a consistent measurement framework
