Independent research center · Founded 2025

Research on persuasion in social media

GCDIR studies when content from social media creators changes the behavior of their audiences. Its main data resource links profiles of creators to the conversion and sales records of their commercial campaigns.

Approach

Most research on social media influence works with the numbers platforms make public: views, likes and shares. They are easy to collect, but they say little about whether anyone was persuaded.

GCDIR works with records of what audiences did after a campaign, such as purchases and sign-ups. Each campaign is linked to a profile of the creator who ran it, covering personality, values and communication style. That makes it possible to ask which creators change behavior and to check the answer against what happened.

Research

The Center’s main program studies persuasion by social media creators. Four related areas are also on its agenda.

Main program

The psychology of persuasion in creator-mediated environments

The program relates psychological profiles of creators to what their audiences did after a campaign, to find out which creators persuade and why.

Current project

Ongoing2025–

Birds of a Feather or Digital Echo Chambers: Congruency in Influencer Marketing

Do audiences respond more to creators who resemble them? The project measures psychological and demographic similarity between creators and their audiences and relates it to engagement and conversions in commercial campaigns.
