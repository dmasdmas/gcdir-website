Our approach

Outcomes, not proxies

The phenomena that matter most in digital influence — how trust is formed, how exposure turns into belief and behavior — happen at scale, in commercial environments, inside systems researchers rarely get to observe. Much of the field has therefore relied on what is publicly visible: likes, shares and impressions.

GCDIR links creator-level psychometric profiles to measured downstream behavior from commercial campaigns, including conversions and revenue. That makes it possible to study persuasion and behavioral change directly, to academic standards.

How our data works

What research usually measures

  • Likes
  • Shares
  • Impressions

Engagement proxies: publicly observable, but only loosely tied to persuasion.

What GCDIR measures

  • Conversions
  • Revenue
  • Transaction-level outcomes
  • Creator psychometrics

Validated behavioral outcomes, linked to the psychology of the creators who drove them.
