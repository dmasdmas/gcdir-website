01

Independence

We choose our own research questions and draw our own conclusions. Our data partners, currently Trudy and TikTok, provide data access and applied context. They do not decide which questions we study, how results are interpreted, or whether they are published.

Any study that evaluates a data partner’s products is pre-registered, with its pass rule fixed before anyone looks at the outcome data.

03

Pre-registration and publication

Before we look at outcomes, we write down the question, the data and the rule that decides whether a hypothesis passes, and we date it. Any analysis we didn’t plan in advance is labeled exploratory.

We will publish the results of every pre-registered study, including null and negative findings. A study that fails its pre-registered test is reported as a fail.

04

Partner review

Before submission, a data partner may review a manuscript for one purpose only: to spot confidential business information or personal data. Partners cannot change findings or how they are interpreted, and they have no right to stop publication.

05

Data protection

  • We work with pseudonymized or aggregated data, under written data-use agreements with each data partner.
  • We don’t publish individual-level data. External researchers work with data only in environments agreed with the data partner.
  • Processing follows applicable data-protection law, including the GDPR where it applies, and the terms under which the data were first collected.
06

Ethics review

Studies that collect new data from people, such as surveys or experiments, are reviewed by an ethics committee at a collaborating university before any data are collected.

07

Funding

We don’t accept funding that depends on the result of a study. Every publication discloses its funding and in-kind support, including data access and staff time from industry partners.

08

Corrections

When we find an error in published work, we correct it in the open, with a dated note saying what changed and why.
