01

Independence

GCDIR chooses its own research questions and draws its own conclusions. Data partners, currently Trudy and TikTok, provide data access and applied context. They do not decide which questions we study, how results are interpreted, or whether they are published.

Studies that evaluate a data partner’s products are always pre-registered, with their pass rules fixed before any outcome data are examined.

03

Pre-registration and publication

Before we look at outcomes, we write down the question, the data, and the rule that decides whether a hypothesis passes, and we date it. Analyses that were not planned in advance are labelled as exploratory.

We publish results whatever they show, including null and negative findings. A study that fails its pre-registered test is reported as a failure.

04

Partner review

Before submission, a data partner may review a manuscript for one purpose only: to identify confidential business information or personal data. Partners cannot change findings or their interpretation, and cannot prevent publication.

05

Data protection

  • We work with pseudonymized or aggregated data, under written data-use agreements with each data partner.
  • Individual-level data are never published. External researchers work with data only in environments agreed with the data partner.
  • Processing follows applicable data-protection law, including the GDPR where it applies, and the terms under which the data were originally collected.
06

Ethics review

Studies that collect new data from people, such as surveys or experiments, are reviewed by an ethics committee at a collaborating university before data collection begins.

07

Funding

GCDIR does not accept funding that depends on the results of a study. Every publication discloses its sources of funding and in-kind support, including data access and staff time from industry partners.

08

Corrections

When we find an error in published work, we correct it openly, with a dated note explaining what changed and why.
